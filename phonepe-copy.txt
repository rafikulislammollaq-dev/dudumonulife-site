APP NAME
Indian Guy: Rich Simulator

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Build an Indian life from scratch — chai tapri to penthouse. Jobs, gaadi, ghar, reels, manhwa and Bubu. No login, plays offline, one choice, one life.

DESCRIPTION
Indian Guy: Rich Simulator is a Hindi-English life simulator where you raise one character from a small beginning to a life you actually built — and you decide the order you do it in.

CHOOSE YOUR GUY
Play as Dudu or Monu. One choice, one life. Close the app and it opens straight back into the same life, exactly where you left it — no re-choosing every launch.

LIFE AND MAZA, NOT HP
Every month of your life moves two meters: LIFE (how well you are doing) and MAZA (how much you are enjoying it). Grind income and ignore Maza and your guy burns out. Chase fun and ignore money and the bills catch up. Balance is the game.

WORK YOUR WAY UP
Start at street jobs — car wash, chai, small shifts — and climb into offices, businesses and the big roles. Each job pays differently and demands differently, and partial effort gives partial pay.

ROAD, SEA AND AIR
50 vehicles. Buy the one you actually want and it shows up in your world, not in a menu. Two-wheelers, cars, and the sky-and-water toys for when you have made it.

PROPERTY THAT EXISTS IN THE SCENE
30 homes, and the ones you buy are physically there. Partners you get are there with you. Nothing is a toggle icon.

MANHWA, REELS AND THE FAME LOOP
Your guy gets screen time: manhwa episodes, reels and sponsor slots. Fame pulls events, and events pull money and trouble in equal measure.

SAVE BUBU
Bubu is the one person who calls at 2 a.m. When she is in danger, the timer is real and your next move matters — 200 rescue missions. Losing her is not a death screen, it is a consequence.

NO DEATH SCREEN, JUST CONSEQUENCES
Fail and your life does not restart to zero. Your meters drop, doors close for a while, and you get to work back up from where you fell.

PLAY OFFLINE
The city runs without internet. Come back after a few hours and your properties pay out what they earned while you were away.

ADVERTISING, AND WHY IT IS HERE
The game is free and made by one person with no budget. Ads are opt-in: every reward button says AD, you only see one by tapping it, and full-screen ads appear at natural breaks — never during a video, never during the Bubu rescue timer, never in your first minute.

No purchase, no account, no login, and your save never leaves your phone.
