Privacy Policy

Indian Guy: Rich Simulator  ·  Android package com.dudumonulife.app
Last updated: 1 November 2026

This game is made by an independent developer. It has no account, no login and no game server. Everything you do in the game is written to a save file on your own phone and never leaves it. The only thing that communicates over the network after launch is the advertising SDK described below, because the game is free and ads are how it is paid for.

1. What stays on your device

Your progress — the character you chose, your Life and Maza meters, money, job, vehicle and home ownership, the manhwa episodes you have watched, and your ad-offer timers — is stored in one file:

  • <app data>/dudu_monu_life_save.json plus one backup copy .bak

Deleting the app or choosing RESET LIFE inside the game removes that file permanently. It is never uploaded, synced or backed up to anything we control.

2. What the advertising SDK collects

Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), which is an ad mediation layer, and the fill in that layer currently comes from Unity Ads. Those SDKs run on your device and collect information we do not see and cannot control:

Collected by the ad SDKWhy
Google Advertising ID (GAID)To recognise your device for ad delivery and frequency capping
IP addressTo route the ad request and derive rough, city-level location
Device model, manufacturer, OS version, screen size and density, language, carrier, network typeTo pick an ad that renders correctly on your phone
App package name and versionTo match the request to this app's ad unit
Ad interaction events: request, impression, click, completion, reward earned, revenue per impressionBillings and reporting for the developer
Crash and diagnostic logs from the SDKStability of the ad stack

The game itself asks Android for INTERNET and the advertising identifier only. It does not collect or request: your name, phone number, email, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, precise GPS location, calendar, or call log. There is no in-app purchase and no chat with other players.

3. Your choices

  • Reset or limit the advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or turn it off completely (google.com/settings/ads). Ads will still appear, just less relevant ones.
  • Unity's privacy policy and opt-out: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • ironSource / LevelPlay privacy policy: is.com/privacy-policy
  • Every rewarded ad in this game is opt-in. Nothing plays an ad unless you tap a button that says AD. Interstitials appear only at natural breaks — never during a video, never during the Bubu rescue timer, never in the first minute of a session.

4. Children

This game is not directed at children under 13 (and not at children under 16 in the EEA/UK). It is not marked child-directed in any ad network, so interest-based advertising is served under the general, non-child-directed configuration. If you believe a child under 13 has installed it, contact us below and we will help you remove it and request deletion of any ad-SDK data from the relevant vendor.

5. Data retention

We retain nothing, because nothing reaches us. Ad vendors retain their own logs under their own policies, linked above. If we ever add a server feature, this page will be updated before the build that uses it is released.

6. Changes to this policy

Any change gets a new date at the top of this page. This page is the authoritative version and is hosted at the URL that was submitted with the app listing.
